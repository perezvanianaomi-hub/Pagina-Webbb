Verb tenses

Four basic tenses
Tense Structure Example
Simple present subject + verb (+ s) She studies English every day.
Simple past subject + verb in past We watched a movie yesterday.
Simple future subject + will + verb I will travel next summer.
Present continuous subject + am/is/are + verb-ing They are playing soccer now.
